Where we focus

Two families of business, both of which we have run.

We invest where we have direct operating experience, because that is what makes us useful after closing rather than only at it. Between us that covers software and services on one side, and consumer and physical goods on the other.

Knowledge and services

01

B2B software and data

Established products with real customers and recurring revenue, where the roadmap has outrun the infrastructure around it.

02

Compliance and regulated services

Businesses whose product is getting something exactly right, on a deadline, under rules that do not move. Sticky, recurring, and unusually document-heavy.

03

Professional and business services

Firms where expertise is delivered by people and capacity is the constraint on growth. We started our careers in this model and know how it makes money.

This is where AI moves the numbers most, because labor is not a line in the cost structure — it is the product. It is also where we built: Participate Systems, Performics, Bazaarvoice, Conversant, AdGooroo, and Alliance Advisors, a proxy solicitation and compliance services firm where Alan sits on the board.

What connects them

Industry is the second question.

The first is whether the business has the shape we can help. That shape is consistent across both families: revenue that recurs or re-occurs, a meaningful volume of work that repeats, and a process nobody has had the time to fix because there has always been something more urgent.

All three of us began our careers in strategy consulting — Bain, BCG and Monitor — before spending three decades operating. That combination is why we can read a business quickly and why we are more interested in what is actually happening inside it than in what sector it files under.

What we look for

The specifics.

  • $1MM or more in annual EBITDA
  • Strong brand equity within a defined market
  • Recurring or re-occurring revenue
  • A diversified customer base
  • Clear AI application opportunities across sales, operations or cost structure

Our preference is to acquire a majority stake, typically 60 to 85 percent, so founders keep meaningful equity and participate in the upside of what we build together. We limit our portfolio to a small number of companies so that every partnership gets our full attention.

How we work

Four things that follow from the structure.

  • We invest our own capital Which gives us flexibility on holding periods, deal sizes and structures, and means the timetable is a judgment about the business.
  • We can reach substantial additional capital Through a network built over twenty years of investing together, brought in where a transaction warrants it.
  • We close quickly Because we have done this before and know what we need to see. Diligence is thorough and it is not slow.
  • We do few deals Which is what allows us to be a genuinely engaged partner to each portfolio company rather than a name on a cap table.
