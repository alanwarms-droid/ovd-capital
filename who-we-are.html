Leadership

We're operators who became investors — not the other way around.

Alan Warms, Jamie Crouthamel and Gary Leff have collectively founded, scaled and exited more than a dozen businesses across technology, digital commerce, consumer products, food and beverage, hospitality, and manufacturing. Alan has been advising enterprise AI and machine learning startups since 2017.

We know what it takes to run a business — and we know what AI can actually do for one.

Our extensive experience operating, scaling and deploying leading-edge technology lets us work as a long-term, valued partner to management. We limit our partnerships so that each portfolio company gets our full attention, and we invest our own capital, which gives us flexibility on holding periods, deal sizes and structures.

Alan and Gary also run OVD Advisory, which delivers the same assessment we run in Phase 1 as a standalone engagement for companies we have no ownership interest in.

The partners

Alan Warms

Principal LinkedIn →

Alan launched OVD Capital in January 2026, after running Participate Capital from 2005 to 2025, and has played key roles in acquiring, investing in and founding more than ten businesses over the last twenty years. Recent investments, alongside Jamie, include Alliance Advisors and Hydro Flask, exited to Helen of Troy.

He has been an advisor to Tensility Venture Partners, a seed-stage venture capital firm focused on enterprise AI and machine learning startups, since 2017. Alan is a member of the Global Advisory Board of the Kellogg School of Management and sits on the boards of Alliance Advisors and Honest Game.

Alan founded BuzzTracker and sold it to Yahoo! in September 2007, where he subsequently ran Yahoo! News, Technology and Education. He was an investor in and publisher of RealClearPolitics from 2005 to 2007, and in 1997 founded Participate Systems, the premier provider of online community software and services to the Global 2000 — social media, before anyone called it that — selling it to OutStart in 2004 and ultimately to IBM.

Alan worked as a strategy consultant with The Boston Consulting Group from 1992 to 1996, and as an engineer on new manufacturing technologies at Kraft General Foods from 1988 to 1990. MBA, J.L. Kellogg School of Management, Northwestern University; B.S. in Electrical Engineering, Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute. He lives in Chicago.

Jamie Crouthamel

Principal LinkedIn →

Jamie has spent more than twenty-five years at the intersection of technology, digital commerce and operating investment — founding, building and scaling companies at the leading edge of the emerging internet economy.

In 1998 he founded Performics, which became one of the country's leading affiliate and search marketing firms, and served as President and CEO through its sale to DoubleClick in 2004. He was a pre-Series A investor and advisor in Bazaarvoice, the leading online ratings and reviews platform, through its IPO in 2012; a pre-Series A investor and director of AdGooroo, a search advertising intelligence platform sold to WPP in 2012; and a board member of Conversant, formerly ValueClick, a pioneer in data-driven digital advertising acquired by Alliance Data Systems in 2014.

Jamie was co-investor and co-board member with Alan in Hydro Flask. His other investments alongside Alan include EatStreet, where he was a director from 2014 to 2022, and Revology Cars, a Ford- and Shelby-licensed manufacturer of new reproduction 1965–68 Mustangs, where he serves as director.

Since 2005 Jamie has operated through Old Town Capital, his Chicago-based operating investment firm. MBA and Master's in Manufacturing Management, Kellogg School of Management, Northwestern University; B.S. in Mechanical Engineering, Drexel University. He lives in Chicago.

Gary Leff

Principal LinkedIn →

Gary has founded, scaled and invested in consumer, hospitality, gaming and food and beverage businesses for more than thirty years, with multiple successful exits. He has played lead roles building high-performing platforms in regulated gaming and experiential retail, including the creation and rapid growth of Laredo Hospitality Ventures, which Alan backed and which scaled to 57 distributed gaming locations in under five years before being acquired by the largest distributed gaming company in the country at the time.

Earlier he founded Stir Crazy, also with Alan as an investor — a private-equity-backed Asian casual dining concept that grew to twelve locations across major U.S. markets and was acquired in 2006. He also co-founded and led the development and operations of Hatch, a leading cannabis dispensary company in the Illinois market.

Gary has been an investor in and advisor to Rivers Casino since 2009, contributing to the strategy and branding of the guest experience at one of Illinois' top-performing casinos, and has advised on several other projects for Rush Street Gaming. He was an investor and active board member of More Than Gourmet, a leading manufacturer of made-from-scratch classic French stocks and sauces, working closely with management to more than double sales and increase profitability, leading to a successful exit via a management-led recapitalization in 2016.

Gary began his career in strategy consulting at Bain & Company and Monitor Group. MBA, J.L. Kellogg School of Management, Northwestern University; B.S. in Economics, The Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania. He lives in Chicago.
