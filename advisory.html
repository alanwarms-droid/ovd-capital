A practical starting point for AI

Most management teams understand that AI will affect their industry. The harder question is where to begin. There are hundreds of tools, rapidly changing capabilities and no shortage of vendors promising transformational results.

OVD Advisory was created to help leadership teams step back from the noise and answer a more useful question: where can AI, automation and better use of data create the greatest economic value for this business?

The AI Opportunity Assessment

Designed for established companies that want a business-led roadmap before committing significant time or capital to implementation. The work typically examines opportunities across both revenue growth and operating efficiency.

  • Revenue and growth Lead generation, sales productivity, conversion, customer service, retention, pricing, product development and new revenue opportunities.
  • Operations and margin Workflow automation, labor productivity, quality control, scheduling, procurement, inventory, finance, reporting and administrative processes.
  • Knowledge and decision support Finding and using institutional knowledge, improving access to company data, accelerating analysis, and helping employees make better and faster decisions.
  • Customer and employee experience Removing repetitive work, shortening response times and making it easier for customers and employees to get the information they need.

How the assessment works

  1. 01 Understand the business Interview leadership and key functional owners to understand the company's strategy, growth objectives, workflows, pain points and economics.
  2. 02 Review the current environment Evaluate the existing technology stack, data sources, core systems and relevant operating procedures.
  3. 03 Identify and prioritize opportunities Develop a list of potential AI and automation use cases and rank them based on likely economic impact, implementation difficulty, data requirements, organizational readiness and risk.
  4. 04 Research the market Determine whether the best path is likely to be an existing software product, an enhancement to a current platform, a specialized implementation partner or a custom solution.
  5. 05 Build the roadmap Deliver a prioritized action plan that distinguishes near-term opportunities from longer-term initiatives and makes clear what would be required to move forward.

What clients receive

  • A prioritized inventory of AI and automation opportunities
  • Estimated business impact and implementation effort for each major opportunity
  • Identification of required data, systems and process changes
  • Assessment of relevant off-the-shelf solutions and potential implementation approaches
  • A recommended sequence of initiatives, including practical quick wins where appropriate
  • Guidance on potential implementation partners and next steps

Independent of an investment transaction

An advisory engagement does not require — or presume — an investment relationship. The objective is to give management a useful, independent roadmap. In many cases, the work ends there.

Representative projects

Luxury travel advisory

Twelve opportunities, and a constraint that was not technology

A midsized luxury travel advisory firm wanted to double sales over five years while remaining relevant to increasingly consolidated premium travel partners. The company had more than thirty years of proprietary information spread across seven systems, making it harder to train new advisors and inefficient for experienced advisors to plan trips.

OVD conducted interviews with senior team members, reviewed the technology stack and operating procedures, and researched available AI products and implementation options. The assessment identified twelve opportunities where AI and automation could improve sales productivity, operating efficiency and advisor effectiveness, centering on a retrieval-augmented knowledge base that could give the "gold standard" answer to any travel inquiry from the firm's own proprietary data and real-time sources. OVD then helped the company solicit and compare proposals from five implementation firms.

Just as important, the assessment concluded that technology was not the company's most important near-term constraint. The higher priority was bringing in training resources to recruit and onboard new advisors. That conclusion reflects how we approach the work: the goal is not to recommend more AI. It is to help management decide what will create the most value.

Financial services

From guesswork to predictive advantage, in weeks

A mid-sized financial-services firm could not reliably forecast revenue, size its market or track its share. We combined three years of revenue data with AI analysis of filings and market feeds, which gave leadership its first defensible view of opportunity and share.

We then built a fleet of AI agents that watch the market, flag new opportunities and anticipate demand. The six-week pilot succeeded and the agents are now in production. The result is smarter capital allocation and a sharper, more predictive sales pipeline.
