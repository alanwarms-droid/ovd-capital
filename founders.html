For founders

You've spent 20 years building something real. AI doesn't have to be something that happens to you.

You've seen the headlines. You know AI is changing your industry. You probably have a sense of where it applies to your business — and a growing feeling that if you don't act, someone else will.

The bind

But you're busy running a company.

You don't have an AI team. And you're not going to bet 20 years of work on a technology experiment.

That's exactly the problem OVD Capital was built to solve. We bring the capital and the operating experience to do this properly, and you keep a real stake in what it produces.

How the partnership works

We buy a majority stake. You keep a meaningful minority.

Typically 70/30 or 85/15, negotiated on the facts of the business.

You take real money off the table — a meaningful liquidity event that recognizes what you've built. But you keep skin in the game as we grow it together. This isn't a buyout. It's a partnership with capital.

Why now

The window is still open, but it's narrowing.

Businesses that deploy AI effectively over the next two to three years will have structural cost and growth advantages that are very hard for competitors to close. We want to help you be one of them, not catch up to them.

Is this for you?

This conversation might be worth having if:

  • Your business generates $1MM or more in annual EBITDA
