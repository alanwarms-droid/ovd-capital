Where we focus

Four verticals where our team has direct operating experience.

We invest in and work with businesses in industries we have actually run. That constraint is deliberate: it is what lets us be useful after closing rather than only at it.

01

Food & Beverage

Specialty manufacturers, regional brands and food service.

02

Outdoor & Consumer Products

Branded lifestyle and consumer goods companies.

03

Consumer Manufacturing

Physical product manufacturers with established distribution.

04

Specialty Retail & DTC

Omnichannel specialty retailers and direct-to-consumer brands.

What we look for

The shape of a business we can help.

  • $1MM or more in annual EBITDA
  • Strong brand equity within a defined market
  • Recurring or re-occurring revenue
  • A diversified customer base
  • Clear AI application opportunities across sales, operations or cost structure

Our preference is to acquire a majority stake, typically 60 to 85 percent, so founders keep meaningful equity and participate in the upside of what we build together. We limit our portfolio to a small number of companies so that every partnership gets our full attention.

How we work

Four things that follow from the structure.

  • We invest our own capital Which gives us flexibility on holding periods, deal sizes and structures, and means the timetable is a judgment about the business.
  • We can reach substantial additional capital Through a network built over twenty years of investing together, brought in where a transaction warrants it.
  • We close quickly Because we have done this before and know what we need to see. Diligence is thorough and it is not slow.
  • We do few deals Which is what allows us to be a genuinely engaged partner to each portfolio company rather than a name on a cap table.
