title: Where We Focus — OVD Capital desc: Two areas where our team has direct operating experience: knowledge and services businesses, and consumer and physical goods.
Where we focus

Two kinds of business, both of which we have run.

We invest where we have direct operating experience. That is what makes us useful after closing rather than only at it, and it is the honest limit on where we can help.

Knowledge and services

Software, compliance and regulated services, and professional and business services. Businesses where expertise is the product and people deliver it — where capacity is the constraint on growth, work recurs at volume, and the day is consumed by coordination, review and getting things exactly right on a deadline.

Consumer and physical goods

Food and beverage, branded consumer and outdoor products, consumer manufacturing, and specialty retail and direct-to-consumer. Businesses where the discipline is consistency across many sites, a supply chain that does not leak margin, and demand you can actually forecast.

What we look for

  • $1MM or more in annual EBITDA
  • Strong brand equity within a defined market
  • Recurring or re-occurring revenue
  • A diversified customer base
  • Clear AI application opportunities across sales, operations or cost structure

Our preference is to acquire a majority stake, typically 60 to 85 percent, so founders keep meaningful equity and participate in the upside of what we build together. We limit our portfolio to a small number of companies so that every partnership gets our full attention.

How we work

  • We invest our own capital Which gives us flexibility on holding periods, deal sizes and structures, and means the timetable is a judgment about the business.
  • We can reach substantial additional capital Through a network built over twenty years of investing together, brought in where a transaction warrants it.
  • We close quickly Because we have done this before and know what we need to see. Diligence is thorough and it is not slow.
  • We do few deals Which is what allows us to be a genuinely engaged partner to each portfolio company rather than a name on a cap table.
