For founders

You've spent 20 years building something real. AI doesn't have to be something that happens to you.

You've seen the headlines. You know AI is changing your industry. You probably have a sense of where it applies to your business — and a growing feeling that if you don't act, someone else will.

But you're busy running a company

You don't have an AI team. And you're not going to bet 20 years of work on a technology experiment.

That's exactly the problem OVD Capital was built to solve. We bring the capital and the operating experience to do this properly, and you keep a real stake in what it produces.

How the partnership works

Typically 70/30 or 85/15, negotiated on the facts of the business.

You take real money off the table — a meaningful liquidity event that recognizes what you've built. But you keep skin in the game as we grow it together. This isn't a buyout. It's a partnership with capital.

What each side brings

What we bring

  • Capital to fund AI investments without straining your operations.
  • A hands-on team with direct experience deploying cutting-edge technologies in real businesses.
  • Operational expertise across software, compliance and professional services on one side, and food and beverage, consumer products, manufacturing and retail on the other.
  • A network of partners, talent and advisors that opens doors.
  • Both sides of an exit — we know what drives valuation multiples, because we have been there.

What you bring

  • Domain knowledge and customer relationships no outsider can replicate.
  • A proven business with real cash flow and a track record.
  • Continued involvement in the areas where your expertise matters most.

Why now

Businesses that deploy AI effectively will build structural cost and growth advantages that are very hard for competitors to close. We want to help you be one of them, not catch up to them.

Is this for you?

  • Your business generates $1MM or more in annual EBITDA
  • You've been thinking about AI but haven't had the time, team or capital to act
  • You want to take some chips off the table without fully walking away
  • You want a partner with real operating experience, not just a financial sponsor
