For founders

A partner for the next chapter.

You have spent years building the business. Our role is to respect that foundation and help create more options for what comes next.

A transaction can accomplish more than one goal

For many founders, bringing in an investment partner is not simply about selling a company. It can be a way to create personal liquidity, reduce concentration, bring additional resources into the business and continue participating in the upside of its future growth.

What each side brings

What we bring

  • Capital for growth, acquisitions, technology, facilities and other strategic investments
  • Decades of direct experience building and operating businesses
  • Perspective on strategy, organization, sales, operations and capital allocation
  • Experience recruiting and working with senior management teams
  • M&A experience on both the buying and selling sides
  • A network of executives, advisors, lenders, technology partners and co-investors
  • A long-term orientation and meaningful personal capital invested alongside our partners

What you bring

  • Deep knowledge of the customers, market and competitive landscape
  • The relationships and reputation that took years to build
  • A culture that has helped the business succeed
  • The practical knowledge of what works — and what does not — inside the company
  • A point of view about the future of the business and what you want the next chapter to look like

How we think about working together

  • Listen first We expect the founder and management team to know the business better than we do. Our first job is to understand.
  • Agree on priorities We want a shared view of the opportunities, risks and investments that matter most.
  • Be useful We get involved where our experience or network can help and avoid creating work simply for the sake of being involved.
  • Respect the culture Growth often requires change, but not every source of value can be captured on a spreadsheet. We pay attention to the people, habits and relationships that make a business work.
  • Stay aligned Meaningful continuing ownership can keep founders, management and OVD focused on the same long-term outcome.

There is more than one way to start

Some business owners are ready to discuss a capital partnership. Others want to get to know us first, compare ideas or explore a specific strategic issue. An initial conversation does not require a process, a pitch deck or a commitment.
