For founders

The structure is familiar. What matters is who is on the other side of it.

A majority recapitalization with meaningful rollover is standard, and any owner running a process will see several of them. What separates one from another is whether the retained stake is worth more in five years — which depends entirely on what the buyer does with the business after closing.

Structure

Terms.

OVD Capital typically takes a majority position and the founder retains a meaningful minority. Arrangements have looked like 70/30 or 85/15, and every deal is negotiated on its own facts.

Which makes the plan the negotiation that matters. Where the retained stake is the point, it is worth being precise well before signing about what is actually going to be done with the business, by whom, and on what timetable.

What comes with it

  • Funding for modernization, so that the work does not compete with operations for cash.
  • Principals who have run companies through this exact kind of change, and have done it repeatedly.
  • A plan that frequently already exists, written with the management team before any transaction was discussed.
The capital

The firm invests its own money.

OVD Capital commits its own capital alongside partners it has invested with for twenty years, and brings in co-investment where a transaction warrants it. It has been doing this across technology, consumer products, hospitality and manufacturing since 2005.

The practical consequence for a founder is that the holding period is a commercial judgment about the business. The firm holds while the plan is working and sells when selling is right, which is a different proposition from a buyer whose timetable is set elsewhere.

It also means the firm is selective. It looks closely at a small number of businesses each year and moves deliberately on the ones it wants.

Fit

What the firm looks for.

The common shape is a company constrained by infrastructure rather than by demand: substantial hours going into work that repeats, knowledge held by a few senior people rather than in any system, and the same information re-entered across three or four applications. Sound businesses with old machinery.

Fit is established on the first call, in both directions.

How it usually starts

Often with work rather than a term sheet.

OVD Advisory, the firm's sister company, spends two to three weeks inside a business establishing where AI creates value and what each initiative returns. Many engagements end there, and should.

Where both parties come to agree on what the business could be worth, and both want to pursue it together, the conversation about capital follows. It is an unusually good introduction: each side has seen how the other works before price is discussed.
