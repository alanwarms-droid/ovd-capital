The problem we solve

Great businesses, running on infrastructure built for a smaller company.

Most founder-owned businesses at this scale already have what startups spend years trying to build: real customers, proven unit economics, and a brand that stands for something. What they lack is the infrastructure to scale efficiently.

Process

Built for ten people

Procedures designed when the whole company fit in one room, still running a business several times that size.

Sales

Running on tribal knowledge

Cycles that depend on what a few people remember, with no pipeline anyone can forecast against.

Operations

Headcount rising with revenue

Nothing in the operation compounds, so every additional dollar of revenue costs roughly what the last one did.

Cost

Never quite optimized

Marketing that doesn't compound and procurement that never gets attention, because there is always something more urgent.

As operators, we know how to implement change and how to deploy technology — including the difference between a spreadsheet and getting it done in the real world.

Why now

The window is open, and it is narrowing.

Businesses that deploy AI effectively over the next two to three years will have structural cost and growth advantages that are very hard for competitors to close. We want to help you be one of them rather than catch up to them.

That is not a reason to move recklessly. It is a reason to move deliberately, starting with the work that pays inside a quarter, on infrastructure solid enough to measure whether it worked.
