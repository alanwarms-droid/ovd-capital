Contact

A straight answer on fit.

A description of the business — what it does, roughly its scale and profitability, and what has prompted the question now — is enough to begin. The reply will say plainly whether there is a fit, including when there is not.

contact@ovdcapital.com

Owners

No materials are required for a first conversation. Where it goes further, the firm will ask for financials on a consistent basis, and will be specific about what it needs and why.

Intermediaries

The firm reviews businesses at $1MM or more of EBITDA, profitable, founder-owned. It moves deliberately and will decline early rather than late.

Co-investors

Co-investment is raised deal by deal alongside partners of twenty years' standing. Conversations with prospective co-investors are welcome ahead of any specific transaction.
