Contact

Start with a conversation.

If you are considering a partner for the next stage of your business, we would be glad to learn more about what you have built and what you want to accomplish.

contact@ovdcapital.com

Business owners

An initial conversation can be informal. We do not need a pitch deck. A general sense of the business, its scale, profitability, ownership and what you are considering is enough to determine whether it makes sense to continue the discussion.

Intermediaries

We are interested in established, profitable, founder- or family-owned businesses generally generating $1 million or more of EBITDA. We value clear communication and aim to give an early view on fit.

Co-investors

We invest our own capital and, when appropriate, invite long-standing co-investment partners to participate alongside us. We are happy to build relationships ahead of a specific transaction.
