Who we are

Operators who became investors — not the other way around.

Three classmates from Kellogg's class of 1992 who have spent the three decades since founding, running and backing companies, a good deal of it together. Between them they have built, scaled and exited more than a dozen businesses across technology, digital commerce, consumer products, hospitality, gaming, food and beverage, and manufacturing.

Two arms

The firm invests. Two of the three also advise.

Capital. All three principals invest their own capital, deal by deal, alongside partners they have known for two decades. There is no fund and no committed pool, which means no ten-year clock and no forced exit, and it means the firm says yes rarely.

Advisory. Alan Warms and Gary Leff run OVD Advisory, which establishes where AI creates value inside operating businesses and then builds it. Many relationships with founders begin there.

The investing is better for the weeks spent inside operating businesses, and the advisory work is better for the investing. They are the same question approached from two directions.

The partners

Alan Warms

Principal · Chicago LinkedIn →

Alan founded BuzzTracker and sold it to Yahoo! in 2007, where he went on to run Yahoo! News, Technology and Education. Earlier he founded Participate Systems in 1997 — the leading provider of online community software to the Global 2000, well before anyone called it social media — and sold it to Outstart, now part of IBM, in 2004. He was an investor in and publisher of RealClearPolitics, and ran Participate Capital from 2005 to 2025.

He has been an advisor to Tensility Venture Partners, a seed-stage fund focused on enterprise AI and machine learning, since 2017 — nine years of examining the technology as an investor rather than as a buyer. He sits on the boards of Alliance Advisors and Honest Game, and on the Global Advisory Board of the Kellogg School of Management.

Alan began his career as a strategy consultant at The Boston Consulting Group and, before that, as an engineer working on new manufacturing technologies at Kraft General Foods. MBA, Kellogg School of Management, class of 1992; B.S. in Electrical Engineering, Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute.

Gary Leff

Principal · Chicago LinkedIn →

Gary has founded, scaled, and invested in consumer, hospitality, gaming and food and beverage businesses for more than thirty years, with multiple exits. He created Laredo Hospitality Ventures and grew it to 57 distributed gaming locations in under five years before its acquisition by the largest distributed gaming company in the country at the time. Earlier he founded Stir Crazy, a private-equity-backed Asian casual dining concept that reached twelve locations across major U.S. markets and was acquired in 2006. He also co-founded and led development and operations at Hatch, a leading cannabis dispensary company in Illinois.

He has been an investor in and advisor to Rivers Casino since 2009, contributing to the strategy and guest experience of one of Illinois' top-performing casinos, and has advised on several projects for Rush Street Gaming. As an investor and board member of More Than Gourmet, a manufacturer of classic French stocks and sauces, he worked with management to more than double sales before a successful exit via management-led recapitalization in 2016.

Gary began his career in strategy consulting at Bain & Company and Monitor Group. MBA, Kellogg School of Management, class of 1992; B.S. in Economics, The Wharton School.

Jamie Crouthamel

Principal · Capital · Chicago LinkedIn →

Jamie has spent more than twenty-five years at the intersection of technology, digital commerce, and operating investment. In 1998 he founded Performics, which became one of the country's leading affiliate and search marketing firms, and served as President and CEO through its sale to DoubleClick in 2004.

He was a pre-Series A investor and advisor in Bazaarvoice through its 2012 IPO; a pre-Series A investor and director of AdGooroo, sold to WPP in 2012; and a board member of Conversant, formerly ValueClick, through its acquisition by Alliance Data Systems in 2014. He was co-investor and co-board member with Alan in Hydro Flask, and is a director of Revology Cars and a former director of EatStreet. Since 2005 he has invested through Old Town Capital, his Chicago-based operating investment firm.

MBA and Master's in Manufacturing Management, Kellogg School of Management, class of 1992; B.S. in Mechanical Engineering, Drexel University.

Twenty-five years at the intersection of technology, digital commerce and operating investment. Invests alongside Alan and Gary, and through Old Town Capital.
